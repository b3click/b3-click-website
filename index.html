ABOUT B3 CLICK

More Than Photographs.

B3 Click is a professional photography and cinematography studio based in Purnea, Bihar, dedicated to turning real moments into timeless visual stories.

From intimate emotions to grand celebrations, our team focuses on capturing every detail, expression and unforgettable moment with a creative and cinematic approach.

We believe wedding photography is not just about taking pictures. It is about preserving the emotions, people and stories that make every celebration unique.
